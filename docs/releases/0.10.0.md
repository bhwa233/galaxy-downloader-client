# 0.10.0 · 小红书视频与下载体验更新

## 变更

- 支持解析小红书单篇视频和创作者主页，主页支持加载更多与批量下载。暂不支持图文笔记。
- 解析结果标明是否使用平台登录态，并在支持的平台提供应用内登录入口。仅在确认有平台字幕时显示字幕下载选项。
- 统一显示各平台提供的宽高、帧率及画质变体。全局设置只决定默认选最高或最低画质，不限制手动选择。下载时重新匹配实际档位，避免平台更换流 ID 导致同画质下载失败。
- 精简任务列表，常用操作集中在每行右侧；完整错误、时间等信息保留在详情中。删除记录默认保留已下载文件。
- 客户端集中提供下载功能；剪辑与转写改由独立的 Galaxy Clipper 承接。旧识别模型和转写记录不会自动删除，详见 [剪辑模式迁移说明](./0.9.0.md)。

## English summary

- Parse Xiaohongshu video posts and batch-download videos from creator profiles. Image posts are not supported.
- Show the parsing session status, offer in-app sign-in where supported, and show the subtitle option only when platform subtitles are confirmed.
- Display actual video dimensions, frame rate and quality variants across platforms. The highest or lowest quality preference sets the default without restricting manual choices. Downloads match the chosen quality even if the platform changes stream IDs.
- Compact task rows put common actions alongside each task. Full errors and timestamps remain in the details. Deleting a record keeps downloaded files by default.
- The client focuses on downloading. Editing and transcription move to Galaxy Clipper; existing models and transcripts are retained.
